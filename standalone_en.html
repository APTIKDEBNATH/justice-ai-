My landlord in Germany sent me a termination letter. Does he need a reason?
Germany · TenancyAnswered from official sources
In most cases, yes. A landlord needs a legitimate reason to end an open-ended residential lease.

Typical valid reasons are genuine personal use of the home or a serious breach by the tenant, and the reason must be stated in the written notice. The landlord's notice period starts at three months and gets longer the more years you have lived there, while your own notice period is normally three months.
